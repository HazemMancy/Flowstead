**3D symbols and clean stream lines**

- **3D symbols.** Equipment is drawn as lit steel with a soft shadow: vessels and columns shaded as cylinders, pumps, heaters and condensers as round casings, with the liquid in vessels shaded along with them. Material streams are short lit pipes with a solid arrowhead. Recycle, Adjust and Set are tinted, so logic stands apart from equipment. The printed PFD stays flat, as engineering drawings are.
- **Stream lines as on a PFD.** Every line runs only across and down, leaving and entering each port square to the side it's on. Recycle lines return underneath, clear of the equipment in their way.
- **Examples laid out cleanly.** Equipment in flow order, left to right, with each stream level with its port. In the depropanizer, T-102's overhead line no longer crosses its reboiler port.
- **New streams line up.** A stream made from a port starts level with it, and one dropped close to level snaps exactly level.
- **Connection check.** A stream can no longer leave a unit and go back into the same unit, as in HYSYS.
