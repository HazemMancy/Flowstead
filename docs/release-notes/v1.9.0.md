**Control loops**

- **Controllers.** A new Controller in the Logical palette, drawn as an instrument bubble with its tag inside: PIC, TIC, FIC, AIC… taken from what it measures. Choose what it measures (PV), its setpoint (SP) and the specification it moves, and give the moved value's range. It solves to the setpoint and shows its output as a % of that range, like a faceplate; at an end of its range it is saturated and shows red.
- **Signal lines.** Dashed lines join each controller to what it measures and what it moves, on the flowsheet and on the printed PFD.
- **Any number of loops.** Adjusts are no longer limited to one per flowsheet. All controllers and Adjusts are solved together, so loops that affect each other, such as a pressure controller and a temperature controller downstream of the same valve, settle at once. Two loops on the same value, or moving the same specification, are flagged.
- **New example:** gas let-down with control loops (PIC-100 on the valve, TIC-100 on the heater).
- **Updates are in Downloads.** The update the app downloads is now saved in your phone's Downloads folder, so Open Downloads finds it. Tapping the "Download complete" notification still installs it in one step (Android asks you to confirm). Android lets an app start the install on its own only with the "install unknown apps" permission, which is what made Play Protect block 1.8.4 and 1.8.5, so that tap stays yours.
- These are steady-state loops: they find where the plant settles. Dynamic PID response (tuning, upsets over time) will come with dynamic simulation.
