**Level lines at every nozzle**

- **No more steps beside the arrows.** Many nozzles sit a little off their symbol's centre (heaters and coolers slightly up, valves down, the pump outlet up). On flowsheets placed on the grid, the stream arrows ended a few units off level, so lines looked broken at the arrowhead and tail. Arrows and single-feed equipment within 14 units of a nozzle's level are now drawn exactly level with it, on screen and on the printed PFD. Your saved layout and results are unchanged.
- **Dropped equipment stays lined up.** When you drop a symbol, the canvas lines it up with its connections; a second rounding to the grid used to undo that. The position you drop is now the position kept.
