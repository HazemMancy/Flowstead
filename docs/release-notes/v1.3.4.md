**What's new in 1.3.4: update notice**

- **New versions are announced when you open Flowstead.** A short message offers the update with one tap (it downloads in the app, as in 1.3.2), or **Later**. It appears once per opening and at most three times for each new version. Turn it off in Settings → About → "Check for updates when Flowstead opens"; Check for updates still works by hand.
