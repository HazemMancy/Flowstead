**Light by default, readable status bar**

- Flowstead now starts in light mode whatever the phone's setting. Dark (or following the phone) can still be chosen in Settings → Theme.
- The phone's status bar icons (time, Wi-Fi, signal, battery) and navigation bar now follow Flowstead's theme: dark icons on the light app. Before, a phone in dark mode drew white icons over the light app, where they could not be read.
