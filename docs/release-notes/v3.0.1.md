**Solves fast again**

- 3.0.0 made a simple solve slow: two plain streams could take over 13 seconds on a phone, against about a second before. The cause was the new bubble- and dew-point check, which searched for a phase boundary with many extra flashes whenever DWSIM's answer did not check out. The search is gone: a bubble or dew point that does not check out is shown as missing (—), never as a wrong number. Pure compounds (water, steam) take DWSIM's vapour-pressure curve directly.
- Bubble and dew points of streams that did not change are remembered between solves, so editing one part of a flowsheet no longer recalculates them for every stream.
