**Review fixes**

- A relief valve or depressuring case on a vessel with more than one feed takes all of them: a blocked outlet relieves every feed, of their mixed composition, at the vessel's own temperature and pressure (it took the first feed only).
- Depressuring a standing vessel with liquid in its bottom head: the head's volume up to a level is now the ellipsoid's (it was overstated, up to half again at mid-head), so the level and the wetted wall are right.
- Separator sizing: a horizontal vessel's mist pad face area uses the pad's own K (it was 20 % small); the oil–water level is named "Oil–water interface".
- Column sizing skips a stage with no density difference between liquid and vapour instead of giving no diameter.
- Pipe fittings are named for what their values are (Crane TP-410): the 0.78 entrance is inward-projecting (Borda), the 0.25 one rounded, the L/D 14 elbow long-radius; the values are unchanged.
- A pump's or compressor's operating speed and an exchanger's UA, watched or used in the spreadsheet, show as numbers in rpm and W/K (they showed as fractions).
