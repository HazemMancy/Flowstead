**What's new in 1.2.0: a new look**

- **New logo:** one stream between two points, from violet feed to cyan product, on a dark tile. App icon, splash screen and every document carry it.
- **New colours:** paper white and ink black with a signal-orange accent, and a matching dark mode. The HYSYS value colours are unchanged: blue for what you specify, ink for what ThermoDock calculates.
- **New fonts:** Geist for the interface and Geist Mono for numbers.
- **Canvas:** vapour amber, liquid teal-blue, two-phase violet; energy streams dashed red-orange. The Solve button is ink black (white in dark mode).
- Data sheets, PFD and heat and material balance use the new logo and warm neutral tones.
