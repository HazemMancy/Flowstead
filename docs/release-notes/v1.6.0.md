**What's new in 1.6.0: safety analysis, as in HYSYS**

Menu → **Safety analysis**. Every fluid property comes from DWSIM at relieving conditions; Flowstead does the standards' arithmetic and shows which equation it used.

**Relief valves (API 520 / 521 / 526).** Add a relief valve to a separator or tank, give its set pressure and the vessel's dimensions, and list its scenarios:
- **Fire**: heat input on the wetted area (Q = 43.2 F A^0.82 kW, or 70.9 without drainage and fire-fighting), boiling the vessel's liquid at the relieving pressure; or the unwetted-wall method for a gas-filled vessel.
- **Blocked outlet**: the vessel's whole feed, from the solved flowsheet.
- **Control valve failure**: the flow through a fully open valve from its upstream pressure.
- **Thermal expansion** of blocked-in liquid.
- **Other**: any flow and temperature you give.

Each case is sized for vapour, liquid, two-phase (omega method) or flashing liquid as the fluid demands; the largest governs and Flowstead picks the API 526 orifice (D to T, or several valves).

**Inlet and outlet lines (API 520 Part II).** Pick the lines' nominal size and schedule: the inlet pressure loss at the valve's rated flow is checked against 3 % of set pressure, and the built-up back pressure against 10 % (conventional), 30 % (balanced bellows) or 50 % (pilot).

**Depressuring (API 521).** Blow a vessel down through a restriction orifice: pressure, fluid and wall temperatures and flow against time, with the orifice sized to reach 6.9 barg (or half the starting pressure) within 15 minutes if you don't give one. A warning flags temperatures below −29 °C (check the minimum design metal temperature). Fire during depressuring is an option.

**Where Flowstead differs from HYSYS:** you list the relief cases (HYSYS can suggest them); the omega method uses an isenthalpic flash; depressuring treats the contents as one well-mixed fluid and the wall as one lump (its mean temperature, so the fluid temperature is also flagged).
