**Copy streams, compositions by mass or volume, and updates inside the app**

- **Duplicate a stream.** Long-press a stream, or use the button in its editor, to get a new feed stream with all its data. A feed brings its specs and composition; a calculated stream brings its solved temperature, pressure, flow and composition, so you can feed a product to a test unit.
- **Copy from another stream.** In a feed's editor, **Copy from another stream…** lets you pick a stream and tick what to take: temperature, pressure, vapour fraction, enthalpy, molar or mass flow, composition. A calculated stream gives its current results only.
- **Composition in your basis.** Enter and read compositions as mole, mass or liquid volume (ideal liquid at 60 °F, as in HYSYS), each as fractions or percent. The stream still keeps mole fractions for the engine. Mass and volume use the engine's own molecular weights and liquid densities, the same ones the solve uses.
- **Moving symbols no longer re-solves.** Where a symbol sits is no longer part of the model, so arranging the flowsheet doesn't make the active solver run again or mark results out of date. Results saved before this update stay current.
- **Updates download inside the app.** **Update** downloads the new version here, with progress, and checks it against the release's checksum. Tap Android's "Download complete" notification to install it. Flowstead doesn't install anything itself, so it needs no "install apps" permission, which is what Play Protect blocked in 1.8.4 and 1.8.5. The browser download is still there if you need it.
