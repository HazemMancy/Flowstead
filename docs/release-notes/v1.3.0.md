**What's new in 1.3.0: stream results as in HYSYS**

- **Results by phase:** every stream is shown as a HYSYS workbook: Overall, then Vapour, Liquid and Aqueous blocks for the phases present. Same layout on the Results screen, in the results Excel/CSV and in the heat and material balance.
- **New values:** molar enthalpy, standard ideal liquid volume flow (at 60 °F) and vapour standard gas flow (Sm³/h, or MMSCFD in field units).
- **More phase properties:** each phase also shows kinematic viscosity, Z factor, Cp, Cp/Cv, thermal conductivity, surface tension (liquids), molar enthalpy and entropy.
- **Liquid density of mixtures fixed:** DWSIM's default mixed pure-liquid densities by mole fraction, so mixtures of light and heavy components read low (propane/n-decane 614 kg/m³ instead of 675). Flowstead now mixes them by mass for PR, SRK, PR/LK, NRTL and UNIQUAC. Pure liquids are unchanged; mixture densities, two-phase pressure drops and residence times of existing projects change.
- **COSTALD liquid density (optional):** Fluid package → Liquid density → "COSTALD (as HYSYS)". The default stays DWSIM's method with the fix above.
- The new phase properties are shown with either liquid density method.
- Property tables on the flowsheet can show molar enthalpy, standard ideal liquid volume flow and thermal conductivity (single-phase streams for the last).
