**Engineering upgrades**

- Blowdown to API 521's target on the design pressure: give the vessel's design pressure and the orifice is sized to reach the lower of 6.9 barg and half the design gauge pressure in 15 minutes. The design pressure also sets the wall (ASME VIII-1) for the wall temperatures. The result says what set the target. Without a design pressure it is half the starting pressure, as before, with a note.
- Air coolers are sized on a crossflow F: both fluids unmixed, in the number of tube passes you give (Tube passes, in the sizing basis; 1 if empty), worked out exactly. It used the shell-and-tube F before, which is not an air cooler's.
- Compressor curves on the polytropic path: choose Polytropic or Adiabatic for the curve, as the vendor drew it. New curves start polytropic; your saved curves stay adiabatic. Results now show the polytropic head and efficiency too.
- Compressor heads are the true isentropic and polytropic work. DWSIM's own head figures were a few per cent off, so a compressor running on an adiabatic curve now settles at a slightly higher pressure ratio than before.
- A pipe fitting by its K: add "Other fitting" to a pipe's sections and type the K (a vendor's valve, a strainer, a nozzle). DWSIM applies it the way it applies its own K fittings.
- Checked against references: Nusselt's exact crossflow solution and Kays & London's table; the exact formula for passes in counter-series; ideal-gas nitrogen compression; API 521's target and ASME's wall thickness worked by hand; a K of 1 equal to DWSIM's own exit loss.
