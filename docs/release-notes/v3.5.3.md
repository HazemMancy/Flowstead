**Solve backward, as HYSYS**

- A product of a valve, heater, cooler, air cooler, pump, compressor, expander or pipe can be given what it must be: its temperature, pressure, vapour fraction, flow or composition. The feed upstream is found to meet it, for example the wellhead temperature that gives 18 °C after the choke.
- If the line can't be solved that way (too many values given, or a unit that fixes its own outlet), the stream says why.

**Packed columns**

- A column's sizing has a new Internals choice: sieve trays, or one of 149 random and structured packings from DWSIM's catalogue.
- The engine rates the packed bed with DWSIM's own correlations. It gives the diameter for 70 % of flood (or the one you enter), the HETP and bed height, the highest flood and the pressure drop over the bed.
