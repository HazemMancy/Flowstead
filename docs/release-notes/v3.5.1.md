**Line sizing of a stream, and the flowsheet stays in reach**

- Line sizing in a stream's results, as HYSYS's stream analysis: size a line from the stream's solved state without adding a pipe segment. Design tries every size of a schedule against your line-sizing criteria and marks the smallest that passes; rating takes one size or an inside diameter. You get ΔP per length (e.g. bar/km), velocity, ρv² and the Mach number, and the total ΔP over the length you give, all from DWSIM.
- km and mile are length units.


- A unit's or stream's editor, and a sub-flowsheet's sheet, no longer cover the flowsheet: tap another object above the sheet and its editor opens straight away; pan and zoom as usual.
- Drag the sheet's title bar up or down to make it taller or shorter, or right down to leave just its title bar; the arrow minimises it or opens it again; ✕ or Back closes it.
- The selection bar can be dragged anywhere by its handle, so it never hides what you want to tap.
- The dashed line between a stream and a sub-flowsheet block is drawn as a connection is, in horizontal and vertical runs from the stream's head (going in) or to its tail (coming out); it ran at an angle from the stream's middle.
- Pressurization from a vessel or pipe: in a pressurization case choose a fixed volume as the source (a vessel, tank or pipe on the flowsheet). It empties until both sides reach the same pressure; the result gives that equalising pressure and when it is reached, the time to your target (or that the target is above the equalising pressure), and the source's coldest gas and wall. Its wall gives heat by default; tick Adiabatic source for the coldest case.
- A vessel or tank can be what is pressurized, not only pipes.
- The valve or orifice can be one already on the flowsheet: a control valve gives its rated Cv (or the Cv it needs in the solve) and xT, an orifice plate its bore.
- From a separator, the line or vessel filled starts holding the gas the separator sends (its vapour), not its liquid.
