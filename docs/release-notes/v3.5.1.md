**The flowsheet stays in reach**

- A unit's or stream's editor, and a sub-flowsheet's sheet, no longer cover the flowsheet: tap another object above the sheet and its editor opens straight away; pan and zoom as usual.
- Drag the sheet's title bar up or down to make it taller or shorter, or right down to leave just its title bar; the arrow minimises it or opens it again; ✕ or Back closes it.
- The selection bar can be dragged anywhere by its handle, so it never hides what you want to tap.
