**Phase envelopes for wet gas**

- Phase envelope of a stream with water in it (a wet or water-saturated gas, or an oil with some water): DWSIM's envelope routine handles only one liquid, so it followed the water dew line instead of the hydrocarbon dew curve, and the envelope never closed. The envelope is now traced for the hydrocarbons on a water-free basis, as HYSYS users read a wet gas. It closes, with its critical point, cricondenbar and cricondentherm. The curves are labelled "(water-free)".
- The water dew line is a separate curve. Below it, liquid water drops out of the stream. The app also gives the water dew point at the stream's pressure and says whether the stream carries free water. It was checked against IAPWS-IF97 steam tables (within 0.4 K up to 5 bar) and the GPSA water-content correlation (within 0.3 K at 30 and 70 bar).
- Hydrate curves are unchanged. A stream that is mostly water keeps its own envelope.
