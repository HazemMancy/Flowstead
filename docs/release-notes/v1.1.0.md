**What's new in 1.1.0**

- **Process flow diagram as PDF:** the flowsheet on one A3 landscape sheet, centred and scaled to fit, with the project title block and the ThermoDock name.
- **Heat and material balance** as PDF (A3 landscape) or Excel: every stream's source and destination, conditions, flows, phase properties and composition, plus energy streams and equipment duties.
- **Property tables on the flowsheet:** long-press a stream or unit, "Show table on flowsheet", pick the properties; drag the table where you want it. Tables print on the PFD.
- **Title block on every export:** data sheets, PFD, H&MB and results files carry company, project, client, site, prepared/checked/approved, document numbers and revision (flowsheet menu, Project documents).
- **Settings, About ThermoDock:** who built it and the DWSIM engine it runs on, and **Check for updates**, which finds and downloads new releases from now on.
- Line-sizing criteria are now set from the pipe segment editor.
- Fixed: stream density, volume flow, molecular weight, entropy, Cp, viscosity and Z showed "—" on the Results screen and in results exports although the engine had calculated them.
