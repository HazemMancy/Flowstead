**What's new in 1.4.0: equipment that checks itself, as in HYSYS**

Every unit operation is now checked after each solve, and problems show on the unit itself (amber badge for warnings, red for errors, the message at the top of its editor), on the Results screen and in the solve bar ("Converged · 2 warnings").

- **Errors (no numbers shown for the unit and what it feeds):** a valve, pump, compressor or expander with the pressure going the wrong way; a pump fed with vapour; a compressor or expander fed with liquid only; a choked pipe; a heat-exchanger temperature cross (now explained in plain words).
- **Warnings (numbers shown, unit flagged):** liquid in a compressor or expander feed, liquid formed during compression, a two-phase expander outlet, vapour traces in a pump feed, low NPSH available, a heater that cools or a cooler that heats, mixer inlets at different pressures, a very small exchanger approach.
- **New results:** pump NPSH available and feed bubble-point pressure; heat-exchanger minimum approach.
