**What's new in 1.4.0: equipment that checks itself, as in HYSYS**

Every unit operation is now checked after each solve, and problems show on the unit itself (amber badge for warnings, red for errors, the message at the top of its editor), on the Results screen and in the solve bar ("Converged · 2 warnings").

- **Errors (no numbers shown for the unit and what it feeds):** a valve, pump, compressor or expander with the pressure going the wrong way; a pump fed with vapour; a compressor or expander fed with liquid only; a choked pipe; a heat-exchanger temperature cross (now explained in plain words).
- **Warnings (numbers shown, unit flagged):** liquid in a compressor or expander feed, liquid formed during compression, a two-phase expander outlet, vapour traces in a pump feed, low NPSH available, a heater that cools or a cooler that heats, mixer inlets at different pressures, a very small exchanger approach.
- **New results:** pump NPSH available and feed bubble-point pressure; heat-exchanger minimum approach.

**Control valve sizing (IEC 60534).** Every valve now reports the Cv (and Kv) it needs, and says when the flow is choked: cavitating or flashing liquid, or critical gas flow. Enter the installed valve's rated Cv to get its opening; a valve that would need more than 100 % is flagged as too small.

**Heat-exchanger zone analysis.** The exchanger is checked along its length, not just at the ends, so an internal temperature cross in a condenser or reboiler is caught. The minimum approach, where it happens and both temperatures there are shown on the exchanger.

**Pump and compressor curves.** Switch on "Run on a performance curve" in a pump or compressor and type the vendor's points (flow, head, efficiency and, for pumps, NPSH required). The outlet pressure then follows from the curve, and the editor draws the curve with the operating point on it.
- Pumps: run-out is an error, below minimum flow a warning, and NPSH available below NPSH required is an error (cavitation).
- Compressors: surge is an error, a surge margin under 10 % or stonewall a warning.

**Where Flowstead differs from HYSYS:** one generic globe valve (FL 0.9, xT 0.72) instead of a valve library; curves at one speed only, with straight lines between your points.
