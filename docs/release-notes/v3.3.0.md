**DWSIM 10.2.15**

- The engine, on the phone and on the server, moves from DWSIM 10.2.12 to 10.2.15, built from its source with Flowstead's patches as before. About and Help now say "DWSIM 10"; Settings and the engine log show the exact version.
- Gases dissolved in water are closer to the IAPWS G7-04 Henry's constants. Air dissolved in the instrument-air receiver's condensate (example 21) is now within 1.2 % of G7-04 for nitrogen, oxygen and argon; DWSIM 10.2.12 was up to 3.8 % off. Methane dissolved in free water (example 04) moves by about 10 %, which is within the uncertainty of the reference.
- Everything else hardly moves. Only 4 of the 22 examples changed at all, and apart from the gases dissolved in water (and the little gas they give off), no result moved by more than 0.1 %; the largest is a 0.016 K temperature in the line-sizing example. Every independent reference value (NIST, IAPWS, HYSYS) still holds.
- DWSIM 10.2.15 also brings upstream fixes to the two- and three-phase flashes, the column solver and several unit operations.
- Results saved with 10.2.12 stay as they are until you solve again.
