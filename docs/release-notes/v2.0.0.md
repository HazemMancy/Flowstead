**Dynamic simulation**

- **Run your flowsheet through time.** Flowsheet menu → **Dynamics**. Starting from the solved steady state, separators hold liquid and gas, valves pass what the pressures either side drive through them, and the flowsheet settles, swings or runs away as a real plant would.
- **PID controllers.** Every controller acts as a PID in a dynamic run: tune its gain, integral and derivative times. A level controller measures a separator's liquid level; pressure, temperature and flow controllers measure what you choose. Each moves a valve's opening or a heater's or cooler's duty. Switch "Acts in the steady state" off for controllers that belong to dynamics only.
- **Events.** Step a feed's pressure or temperature, a valve's opening, a setpoint, a duty or a controller's mode at any time in the run.
- **Trends.** Each controller's measurement against its setpoint and its output; every separator's pressure, level and temperature; valve openings, flows and duties.
- **Equipment in dynamics:** separators (give their volume and height), valves (sized to their steady flow at half open, or give their rated Cv), feeds and products, heaters, coolers, air coolers, mixers, pumps, compressors and expanders. Columns, reactors, tanks and three-phase separators come later; the app says when a flowsheet has one.
- **New example:** separator under pressure and level control. Open it, then Dynamics → Run: the feed steps from 30 to 33 bara at 30 s and the controllers bring the separator back.
