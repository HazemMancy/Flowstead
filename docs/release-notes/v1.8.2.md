**Flowstead again, with the new look**

The app is called Flowstead again. The ThermoDock name, also the name of another company's product, set off Google Play Protect on many phones. Everything new from 1.8 stays: the logo (two streams meeting at a unit, teal on midnight navy), the navy and teal colours, the Inter typeface, Help & Resources and all the sizing tools. It installs as an update over 1.7.0, 1.8.0 or 1.8.1 and keeps all your projects.

**Updates through your browser.** Flowstead no longer installs its own updates. When a new version is out, **Update** opens the download in your browser; open the file when it finishes and Android installs it over this version. The app therefore no longer asks for permission to install other apps, which Play Protect weighs heavily against apps installed from outside the Play Store.
