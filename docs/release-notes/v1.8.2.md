**Updates through your browser**

ThermoDock no longer installs its own updates. When a new version is out, **Update** opens the download in your browser; open the file when it finishes and Android installs it over this version, keeping all your projects. The app therefore no longer asks for permission to install other apps. Google Play Protect weighs that permission heavily against apps installed from outside the Play Store, and it blocked 1.8.0 and 1.8.1 on some phones. Nothing else changes.
