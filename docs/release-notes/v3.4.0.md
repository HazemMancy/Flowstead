**Sub-flowsheets, as in HYSYS**

- A sub-flowsheet is a block on the flowsheet with a flowsheet of its own inside. Add one with "+ Sub-flowsheet" (top left of the flowsheet), or long-press a unit and choose "Put in a new sub-flowsheet". Tap a block twice, or long-press it, to open it; the bar at the top leads back to the main flowsheet.
- Streams in and out, as HYSYS's Connections: in the block's sheet, add a stream in or out from the free streams around it, or drag a stream onto the block. The stream inside is made for you; join it to the equipment inside.
- Each block uses the parent's fluid package or its own, any package with its own components (edit it in the Fluid screen from the block's sheet). On the parent's package the case solves as one flowsheet, with exactly the numbers it has without the block. On its own package, each stream crosses as a feed defined by the engine's result on the other side, by temperature and pressure or by pressure and vapour fraction (the transfer basis), with its flow and composition.
- Checked: a case with half its units in a block gives the flat case's numbers; water heated in a block on the steam tables needs the duty the IAPWS-IF97 equations give (527.0 kW for 1 kg/s from 25 to 150 °C at 5 bar).
- The PFD prints the main flowsheet with its blocks, then a page for each sub-flowsheet.
