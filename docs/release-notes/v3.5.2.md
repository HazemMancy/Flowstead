**Water with dissolved gas**

- A three-phase separator's water now holds the gases Henry's law gives (CO₂, methane, ethane, nitrogen and the others with a Henry's constant), and those gases are taken from the gas it leaves, so every compound balances across the separator. For a wet gas, the water outlet used to carry far too much ethane and no CO₂.
- Water drawn off a vessel keeps the air or gas dissolved in it at the vessel's temperature and pressure, with no spurious bubble of vapour.
