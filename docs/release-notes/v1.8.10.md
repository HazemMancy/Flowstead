**Faster re-solves**

- **Only what changed is recalculated.** After you change a specification, the solver now keeps the flowsheet it already solved and recalculates from the changed object downstream; everything upstream keeps its results. A feed change ahead of a reactor train went from about 0.46 s to 0.06 s in our tests.
- **Recycles and Adjusts start from the last answer.** A recycle loop that took 18 iterations from scratch converged in 1 after a small change, and every Adjust iteration reuses the flowsheet instead of building it again.
- **Same numbers.** Every benchmark case is checked: the faster re-solve gives the result a solve from scratch gives. Changing the column or reactor setup, the components or the connections still solves from scratch.
