**What's fixed in 1.6.1**

**Depressuring runs.** A vessel holding water with its hydrocarbons stopped after a few seconds (DWSIM's three-phase flash jumps near the water's phase boundary and the pressure solve gave up), and the orifice sizing then kept enlarging the orifice until it timed out. The blowdown now rides over those jumps, every flash reuses one prepared DWSIM stream (the same results, four to six times faster), and the wall can no longer be cooled below the fluid by a strong boiling film. A typical separator with free water now sizes its orifice in about a minute on a server; on the phone allow a few minutes.

**Relief valves give the valve.** The result now leads with the API 526 orifice letter and the smallest standard valve for it (for example 3K4: 3 in inlet × K × 4 in outlet), then the selected and required areas with their equivalent diameters in mm and inches. When more than one T is needed, the right letter is shown for the several valves.

**Straight stream lines.** Connections are drawn as one straight line. A dragged stream or unit settles on the grid and lines up with what it connects to, so its lines come out level or plumb.
