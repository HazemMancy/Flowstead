**Z factor for gases only, and faster with hypothetical components**

- The Z factor (Z = PV/nRT) is now shown only for a vapour phase and for vapour-only streams. Liquid phases no longer show a Z factor, and neither do liquid or two-phase streams. This also covers projects solved by earlier versions. In the PVT experiments, an oil above its bubble point has no gas Z, while a gas condensate above its dew point still shows its Z.
- Cases with hypothetical components solve faster. Each hypothetical's properties (critical constants, acentric factor, Rackett and COSTALD parameters, volume shifts) come from several fits, and these were repeated on every solve. They are now calculated once and reused until the hypothetical's inputs change. On the server the fits took about 90 ms per hypothetical per solve. A repeat solve of a stream with one hypothetical dropped from about 240 ms to about 100 ms, the same as a database compound. The saving grows with the number of hypotheticals, and the phone's engine is slower than the server's.
