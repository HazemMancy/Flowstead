**What's new in 1.3.1: dense-gas fixes**

- **Dense gas is one phase, as in HYSYS.** At high pressure DWSIM's Peng-Robinson flash could split a gas into two copies of the same fluid (a C1–C5 gas at 320 barg and 35 °C showed a vapour fraction of 0.39 where HYSYS shows 1). Such splits, where both "phases" have the same density within 5 %, are now reported as one phase. Real vapour–liquid splits are not affected.
- **No more density of 0.** Near the critical point DWSIM's liquid-density correlations have no value; the phase density is now taken from the equation of state, so phase and stream densities are always reported.
- Both apply to PR, SRK and PR/LK, with either liquid-density method.
