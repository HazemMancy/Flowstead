**What's new in 1.3.1: density fix**

- **Near-critical streams no longer show a density of 0.** At high pressure (for example a rich gas at 320 bar) DWSIM can split the stream into two nearly identical phases, and its liquid-density correlations have no value for that "liquid". The phase density is now taken from the equation of state, so the phase and stream densities are reported (321 kg/m³ for the test gas, the same as the single-phase value). Applies to PR, SRK and PR/LK, with either liquid-density method.
- Vapour fractions of such near-critical streams are DWSIM's and unchanged; they are being compared with HYSYS.
