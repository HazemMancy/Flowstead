**Solve what is ready**

- **One empty stream no longer stops the case.** The solver now calculates everything that is ready and leaves the rest. A stream without conditions or composition, or a unit missing a specification, is left out, together with whatever is downstream of it; everything else is solved.
- **Red for not ready, green for solved.** Objects left out are red on the flowsheet, in their editor and in the results worksheet, with the reason: what they need ("Needs outlet pressure or pressure drop") or what they wait for ("Waits for VLV-100"). Solved objects are green. Tap the solve bar to see the full list and jump to each one.
- Still needed for any solve: a property package and components. Case studies and the DWSIM file export still need the whole flowsheet complete.
