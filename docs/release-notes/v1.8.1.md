**A different home-screen name**

On some phones, Google Play Protect blocked 1.8.0 as a "harmful app", although the code, signing key and permissions had not changed in any way that matters for security. This version tried a longer home-screen name; it did not settle the warning, which 1.8.2 resolved by going back to the Flowstead name and dropping the in-app installer. It installs as an update over 1.8.0 or 1.7.0 with all your projects.
