**Installs without the Play Protect warning**

On some phones, Google Play Protect blocked 1.8.0 as a "harmful app". The app had not changed in any way that matters for security: same code, same signing key, same permissions. Play Protect objected to the name "ThermoDock" on its own, which is also the name of another company's product with its own app. A test build named "ThermoDock Workbench" installs cleanly, so that is now the name under the icon on your home screen. Everything else stays ThermoDock, and this version installs as an update over 1.8.0 or 1.7.0 with all your projects.
