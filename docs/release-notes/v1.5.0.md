**What's new in 1.5.0: an interactive simulator**

This release includes everything in 1.4.0 (unit checks, valve sizing, exchanger zone analysis, pump and compressor curves) and adds:

**Solver Hold / Active.** A switch at the left of the solve bar, saved with each project, as in HYSYS. *Hold* (amber) solves when you press Solve. *Active* (green) solves again a moment after every change you make: type a value, drag a slider or undo, and the results follow.

**What-if sliders.** Tap the slider icon beside any specification to slide it around its current value. The new value is set when you lift your finger; with the Active solver the whole flowsheet re-solves straight away.

**Case studies.** Menu → Case studies. Pick one specification to vary (for example the feed temperature), its range and the number of steps, and the results to record (for example compressor power and separator vapour fraction). Add a second variable to get a family of curves (for example one line per suction pressure). Flowstead solves the whole flowsheet at every point and plots each result against the variable, with a table of the numbers. A point that does not converge is shown as a gap with the reason.

**Plots.** Every plot is in your units; touch or drag on it to read the values.
- Heat exchangers: the temperature–heat flow (T–Q) diagram from the zone analysis, showing where the pinch is.
- Pipes: pressure, temperature, liquid holdup and velocity along the line.
- Distillation columns and absorbers: stage temperatures, vapour and liquid flows, and pressures.
- Streams: "Trace phase envelope" draws the bubble and dew curves with the critical point, cricondenbar, cricondentherm and the stream's own state, plus quality lines (vapour fraction 0.1 to 0.9, your choice) and the hydrate formation curve, with the temperature below which hydrates form at the stream's pressure.

**PVT laboratory.** From any stream: "PVT analysis" runs the standard reservoir-fluid experiments at the reservoir temperature you give:
- Saturation pressure (bubble point for an oil, dew point for a gas condensate).
- Constant composition expansion: relative volume, liquid dropout, Z, density, Y-function.
- Constant volume depletion: liquid dropout, gas produced, gas and two-phase Z.
- Differential liberation: Bo, Rs, oil density, gas gravity.
- Separator test: GOR per stage, Bo, stock-tank oil density and °API.

**Pipe sizes everywhere.** Wherever a pipe diameter is needed, pick the nominal size and schedule (ASME B36.10M / B36.19M) from the same menus as the pipe segment: now also for the line an orifice plate sits in. "Custom inside diameter" still lets you type it.

**What Flowstead adds.** Settings → About → "What Flowstead adds": what Flowstead builds on top of the DWSIM engine, what works like HYSYS, and where it still differs.

**Where Flowstead differs from HYSYS:** the hydrate model is the textbook van der Waals–Platteeuw one (within about 1 K of measured data) and assumes free water with no inhibitor (methanol, MEG) or salt. PVT has no regression to lab data (EOS tuning) yet. A DWSIM flash that returns an impossible phase (zero or negative density) is now reported as an error instead of a number.
