**Flowstead is now ThermoDock**

The app has a new name and a new look; everything else is the same app. It updates in place from 1.7.0 and keeps all your projects.

- **Name:** ThermoDock, the process engineering workbench.
- **Logo:** two streams meet at a unit and leave as one: teal lines and a white node on a midnight-navy tile, on the launcher, the splash screen, in the app and on every data sheet, PFD and export.
- **Colours:** midnight navy and engineering teal, technical blue for what you typed, amber warnings and coral errors, in light and dark themes. Every text colour keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast; filled teal buttons carry navy text for that reason.
- **Typeface:** Inter throughout, with tabular figures so columns of numbers line up.
