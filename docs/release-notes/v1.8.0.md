**A new look**

Flowstead has a new logo, colours and typeface; everything else is the same app. It updates in place from 1.7.0 and keeps all your projects.

- **Logo:** two streams meet at a unit and leave as one: teal lines and a white node on a midnight-navy tile, on the launcher, the splash screen, in the app and on every data sheet, PFD and export.
- **Colours:** midnight navy and engineering teal, technical blue for what you typed, amber warnings and coral errors, in light and dark themes. Every text colour keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast; filled teal buttons carry navy text for that reason.
- **Typeface:** Inter throughout, with tabular figures so columns of numbers line up.

**Tray column sizing.** A distillation column or absorber now sizes too: tap **Size this equipment** in its editor for the diameter and the tangent-to-tangent height. Every tray is checked with its own solved vapour and liquid flows, densities and surface tension (Fair's flooding correlation, as HYSYS's tray sizing), at your tray spacing, design flood, downcomer area, tray efficiency and foaming factor. This completes the equipment sizing: separators, exchangers, heaters, coolers, air coolers, pump and compressor drivers, tanks and columns.

This version carried an interim name, since withdrawn: 1.8.2 is Flowstead again.
