# LinkedIn post — Getting started with Flowstead, episode 2

From the well to the inlet separator, in three and a half minutes. 🎬

Episode 2 of Getting Started with Flowstead takes episode 1's well gas where it really goes, on the real DWSIM engine
in the browser:

🔹 A choke from 70 to 40 bara: 12 °C of Joule–Thomson cooling, and the enthalpy in equals the enthalpy out
🔹 The phase envelope with the hydrate curve: hydrates below 14 °C, only 4 °C of margin, and what 20 wt % methanol buys
🔹 A three-phase inlet separator: gas, condensate and water, with every compound balanced, even the gas dissolved in
   the water
🔹 Sizing it in the Design tab: 1.68 m × 8.4 m horizontal, Souders–Brown, levels and nozzles
🔹 Line sizing of the gas, as HYSYS's stream analysis: why NPS 4 meets the velocity and ρv² limits but not a 2 km line,
   and why NPS 6 does
🔹 Blowdown to API 521: 6.9 barg in 14.6 minutes through a sized orifice, the gas down to −15 °C, the wall above 9 °C

Every number in the video comes from the engine and is checked against a hand calculation in Flowstead's tests.
