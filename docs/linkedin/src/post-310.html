Flowstead
PROCESS ENGINEERING WORKBENCH
Version 3.1

Assays, black oil and phase diagrams, in your pocket

The biggest update since DWSIM 10: characterise crudes, model black oils, draw phase diagrams and keep more of your DWSIM files, all solved on the phone.

T–x–y, P–x–y, ternary, critical point

n-Hexane / n-heptane at 1 atm with Peng–Robinson, as Flowstead's DWSIM engine traces it. Checked against NIST boiling points and Raoult's law.

Petroleum assays

TBP, D86, D1160 or D2887 curves cut into pseudo-components, with light ends. Pour, cloud and flash points of any stream.

Black oil

API, gas gravity, GOR and water cut. Standing and Vasquez–Beggs, with DWSIM's black-oil code corrected in our build.

Thermodynamics tools

Binary and ternary diagrams, true critical point, fluid packages side by side, property charts.

DWSIM files

Pipes with several sections and fittings, Adjusts, controllers and Sets go to and from DWSIM.
