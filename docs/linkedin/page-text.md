## About (1645 characters)

Flowstead is a free, open-source process simulator for process engineers. It runs on Android phones and, since November 2026, in any browser (iPhone, iPad, desktop) with nothing to install: the web version runs the same engine on your own device, with no server, and nothing you type leaves it.

Every number comes from the DWSIM 10.2.12 engine. Flowstead never shows a value the engine did not return, and each result is checked in a test against an independent reference: a hand calculation, steam tables, the standard's worked example, or a HYSYS value.

What you can do with it
• Build and solve steady-state and dynamic flowsheets: separators, compressor and cooler trains, valves, pipelines (Beggs & Brill), columns, reactors, recycles and PID control.
• Pump and compressor curves, valve sizing (IEC 60534), exchanger zone analysis, line and separator sizing (API 14E).
• Phase envelopes with quality lines, hydrate curves and inhibitor doses, wet-gas water dew lines, and a PVT laboratory.
• Safety: PSV sizing (API 520/526), relief scenarios and depressuring (API 521), inlet-loss and back-pressure checks. The fire-case PSV of a wet air receiver reproduces the HYSYS orifice.
• Data sheets, PFD and heat and material balances as PDF or Excel.

Android: signed APKs on GitHub Releases (43 releases so far). Web: https://hazemmancy.github.io/Flowstead/flowsheet.html
Source (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/HazemMancy/Flowstead

Created and maintained by Hazem ElMancy, Oil & Gas Process Engineer, Aspen HYSYS Certified Expert User. Flowstead is a screening and checking tool: confirm design results against your project's approved methods.
