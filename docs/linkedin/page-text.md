## About (1813 characters)

```text
Flowstead is a free, open-source process simulator for process engineers, for steady state and dynamics. It runs on Android phones and in any browser (iPhone, iPad, desktop) with nothing to install: the web version runs the same engine on your own device, with no server, and nothing you type leaves it.

Every number comes from the DWSIM 10.2.12 engine. Flowstead never shows a value the engine did not return, and each result is checked in a test against an independent reference: a hand calculation, steam tables, a standard's worked example or a HYSYS value.

What you can do with it
• Steady-state flowsheets: separators, compressor and cooler trains, valves, pipelines (Beggs & Brill), columns, reactors and recycles.
• Dynamic simulation: run a solved flowsheet through time with PID controllers (level, pressure, temperature, flow), step a feed, a valve or a setpoint, and read the trends.
• Relief and safety: PSV sizing (API 520/526), relief scenarios and depressuring (API 521), inlet-loss and back-pressure checks. The fire-case PSV of a wet air receiver reproduces the HYSYS orifice.
• Phase envelopes with quality lines, hydrate curves and inhibitor doses, wet-gas water dew lines, and a PVT laboratory.
• Pump and compressor curves, valve sizing (IEC 60534), line and separator sizing (API 14E).
• Data sheets, PFD and heat and material balances as PDF or Excel.

Android: signed APKs on GitHub Releases. Web: https://hazemmancy.github.io/Flowstead/ (stream workbench) and https://hazemmancy.github.io/Flowstead/flowsheet.html (flowsheet).
Source (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/HazemMancy/Flowstead

Created by Hazem ElMancy, Oil & Gas Process Engineer and Aspen HYSYS Certified Expert User. Flowstead is a screening and checking tool: confirm design results against your project's approved methods.
```
