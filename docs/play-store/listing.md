## Full description (≤ 4000)

Flowstead is a steady-state and dynamic process simulator for chemical and process engineers, built for the phone.

Draw a flowsheet with your finger, specify streams and unit operations, and solve it on your own Flowstead engine, which runs the open-source DWSIM thermodynamics and unit operations on a server you control.

**Built for engineers who use HYSYS or UniSim at their desk**
• Familiar value colours: blue for what you specified, black for what was calculated, amber for what still needs a specification, red for errors.
• Degrees-of-freedom check before every solve, in plain language.
• Units the way you work: bara and barg (with your site's atmospheric pressure), °C or °F, kgmol/h, kg/h, Sm³/d, MMSCFD and more.

**Unit operations:** material and energy streams, mixer, splitter, heater, cooler, heat exchanger, valve, pump, compressor, expander, two- and three-phase separators, recycle and adjust.

**Property packages:** Peng-Robinson, SRK, Peng-Robinson / Lee-Kesler, NRTL, UNIQUAC, Steam Tables (IAPWS-IF97), GERG-2008 and CoolProp.

**Results you can trust:** every number on screen came from the engine. Missing values show "—" with the reason, and results are marked out of date as soon as you change the flowsheet. The solver log explains failures in plain language.

**Work offline:** edit flowsheets without a connection. A solve that can't reach the engine is queued and runs when you're back online.

**Move work between phone and desk:** import and export DWSIM `.dwxmz` files, and export results to CSV or Excel.

**Requirements:** Flowstead needs a Flowstead engine to solve. It is open source and runs in Docker on any Linux x64 server; see the project page for setup. The app does not include a hosted engine.
